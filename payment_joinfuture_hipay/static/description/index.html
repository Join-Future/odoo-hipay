HiPay by Join Future

Online payments for Odoo in Bahrain and the GCC

Take card, BENEFIT, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments on your Odoo website, portal and invoice payment links. Your customer pays on the secure HiPay checkout, Odoo confirms the order from the HiPay API and from signed webhooks, and you refund in full or in part from Odoo.

What you get

Cards, BENEFIT and wallets

Visa and Mastercard with 3-D Secure, BENEFIT debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, all on one hosted checkout page in English or Arabic.

Exact to the fil

BHD, KWD, OMR and JOD use 3 decimals and SAR, AED, QAR, EGP and USD use 2. Amounts are sent in minor units with no rounding drift.

Items and VAT on the checkout

The checkout lists the order lines with their images, the delivery line and the VAT row, so the customer sees exactly what they pay for.

Verified, not trusted

The return from the checkout is checked against the HiPay API with your secret key, and webhooks are checked with an HMAC-SHA256 signature and a 5 minute window.

Refunds from Odoo

Refund in full or in part from the payment. Refunds made in the HiPay portal are added to Odoo by the webhook, once.

Safe retries

Every payment and refund request carries an idempotency key, so a network retry never charges or refunds twice.

The customer pays on HiPay

Odoo lists HiPay's card and BENEFIT methods at checkout.

The HiPay checkout shows the items, delivery, VAT and total, then sends the customer back to Odoo.

The order is confirmed once HiPay confirms the payment.

Set up in minutes

  1. Ask Join Future for a HiPay merchant account at pay.joinfuture.ai. You get a test secret key at once and a live key after onboarding.
  2. In Odoo, open Invoicing or Website, Configuration, Payment Providers, HiPay by Join Future.
  3. Paste the test secret key (hp_sk_test_...) and set the state to Test Mode.
  4. In the HiPay portal, add a webhook endpoint for https://your-odoo/payment/hipay/webhook and paste its whsec_ signing secret.
  5. When you are ready, paste the live key and its webhook secret and set the state to Enabled.
